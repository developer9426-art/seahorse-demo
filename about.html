Our story

From a handful of distilleries to a global supplier

Seahorse is a leading manufacturer and merchant exporter of 100% pure, therapeutic-grade essential oils and other natural materials. Starting with a handful of distilleries, we now produce over 80% of our own products and source the rest from certified, ethical producers worldwide.

Our central purchasing system lets us offer one of the largest selections of pure essential oils at fair prices, in pack sizes for every need, small or large.
